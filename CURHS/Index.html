Ministry of Science & NMST

Recognition

Ministry of Science & NMST

Chittagong University Research and Higher Study Society (CURHS) was founded in 2019 with the mission to inspire students in research and higher education. From its inception, this science and research-based club has played a significant role in spreading scientific knowledge among the masses by organizing science fairs, research workshops, research internships, and various activities throughout the year, alongside encouraging students to pursue research and higher studies. Recently, in recognition of these efforts, the club has been officially registered as a science organization by the Ministry of Science and Technology and the National Museum of Science and Technology, with registration number: G-247/2023.

The National Museum of Science and Technology, under Law-11, recognizes organizations for their contributions to science-based activities and significant discoveries or advancements in science and technology. With this recognition, Chittagong University Research and Higher Study Society has earned the honor of being acknowledged as a national-level science club for its scientific contributions. This achievement brings us great joy and inspiration.

We aim to continue this progress successfully in the near future. Thank you for being with us on this journey of dreams!
